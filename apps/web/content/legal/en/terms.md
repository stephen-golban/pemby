## 2. What Pemby is

Pemby is AI job-matching software. You apply yourself.

Pemby reads your CV and your answers about where and how you can work. It compares them with public job posts from company career pages and the applicant tracking systems those companies use. When a job can hire from your country and fits your profile, Pemby sends it to you by Telegram, email or web push. When nothing fits, Pemby tells you and shows what blocked the closest jobs.

Pemby can also draft an application kit for a job. A kit has suggested CV bullets, a short cover letter and answers to screening questions. You review, edit and paste them yourself.

## 3. What Pemby is not

- Pemby does not apply to jobs for you. You decide where to apply and you submit every application yourself, on the employer's own site.

- Pemby does not promise that you will get an interview, an offer or a job. No pass, feature or guarantee in these terms is about job outcomes.
