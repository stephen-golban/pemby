# Refund policy

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for Pemby passes. It is part of our Terms of Service. Pemby is run by "SYNCRA" S.R.L., trading as Syncra Studio, registered in Chișinău, Republic of Moldova.

## 1. The short version

- You can get a full refund within 14 days of buying a pass. You do not need to give a reason.
- A refund ends the pass it refunds.
- Passes are one-time payments. They never renew, so there is nothing to cancel.
- To ask for a refund, email hello@pemby.app.

## 2. Refund window

You can ask for a full refund of a pass within 14 days of the date you bought it. We refund the full price you paid, including any tax charged at checkout.

This applies to each purchase separately. If you bought two passes on different days, each one has its own 14-day window.

## 3. How to ask for a refund

Email hello@pemby.app from the email address you used at checkout. Include the date of purchase, or the receipt or order number from your payment receipt.

We reply within 3 business days. Our payment provider then sends the money back to the payment method you used. How long it takes to reach you depends on your bank or card issuer.

## 4. What happens to your pass

A refund ends the pass it refunds.

- If the refunded pass is your only pass, your account returns to the free tier. Your profile, matches and kits stay.
- If you had stacked passes, we remove only the time from the refunded purchase. Your other passes stay.
- Days the no-match guarantee added because of the refunded pass are removed too.
- Kits you made while the pass was active stay in your account.

## 5. After 14 days

After 14 days, we do not refund a pass, except:

- where the law where you live gives you a right to a refund;
- where our payment provider's buyer terms give you a right to a refund;
- where Pemby did not work for you because of a fault on our side that we could not fix.

The no-match guarantee is separate from refunds. If an active pass gives you zero matches for 14 days in a row, we add 14 days to the pass. It adds time. It does not return money.

## 6. Cancellation

Pemby has no subscriptions. A pass is a single payment for a fixed time, and it ends on its own. We never charge you again unless you buy another pass. You do not need to cancel anything.

If you want to stop using Pemby, you can:

- ask for a refund within 14 days, as described above;
- use "Landed a role?" to pause your pass and keep the remaining time for later;
- delete your account from your profile page. If you are still within the 14-day window, ask for a refund before you delete the account.

## 7. Your payment provider

Our payment provider sells passes as merchant of record. It is the seller on your receipt and it processes the refund. Its own buyer terms also apply to your purchase. If you live in the European Union or another country with a legal right to withdraw from online purchases, those rights apply in addition to this policy.

## 8. Chargebacks

Please email us before you open a dispute with your bank. We can usually refund faster than a chargeback takes. If a chargeback is opened, the pass it covers ends while the dispute is open.

## 9. Referral and bonus days

Days from referrals, the no-match guarantee or other rewards have no cash value and cannot be refunded.

## 10. Contact

Email hello@pemby.app. We reply within 3 business days.
